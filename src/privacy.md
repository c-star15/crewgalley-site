# Galley Privacy Policy

*Version 2026-10-26. Takes effect when Galley launches on the App Store and Google Play.*

Galley is a social app for verified airline pilots and cabin crew to meet other crew on layovers. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past. If anything is unclear, write to [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app).

## 1. Who we are

Galley is operated by **its founder**, an individual based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates ("Galley", "we", "us"), postal address on request at [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app). We are the controller of your personal data.

- Privacy questions and requests: [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app)
- Representative in the European Union: being appointed before launch; until then write to [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app)
- Representative in the United Kingdom: being appointed before launch; until then write to [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app)

Galley is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any airline, including yours. We do not share your data with any airline.

## 2. The short version

- We collect the minimum needed to let verified crew make plans at their base and find each other in the same city on the same days.
- Your proof of employment is seen only by our verification team and deleted, usually within 24 hours of the decision.
- Other members see your profile only when you share a meetup, or when you are both on a layover in the same city on overlapping dates, and then only from 2 days before your overlap starts. Before that they see a count, never your name. Nobody can browse a city where they have no layover, crew based in a city never appear on its board, and your home base is always your base city.
- The free days you mark at your base are only ever counted; a count under 3 is never shown. If you also pick what you're up for that day, crew at your base see your name, photo and what you're up for, but only the group you choose: anyone, women only or men only.
- New contacts start with an invite: a plan and a time, no text. Only members who switch invites on can get them, and by default only from crew whose phone shows they've landed in the same city. Your phone does that check; only the city is sent to us, never where you are.
- Your messages are end-to-end encrypted on your phone. We cannot read them, and neither can anyone who gets into our servers.
- We never ask for your hotel, room, flight number, roster or staff number, and our filter catches the common forms of them in public text.
- We do not sell your data, do not show ads, and do not use advertising or cross-app tracking.
- You can export your data or delete your account from Settings at any time.

### 3.2 Data created when you use Galley

- **Verification decisions**: approved, rejected or second proof requested, the reason, the date and which admin decided. No image is stored with it.
- **Identity mark**: when we approve you, the reviewer types the staff number and surname shown on your proof, and our server turns them, with your airline, into a keyed one-way code. The staff number and surname themselves are not stored, and the code cannot be turned back into them; the key that makes it is held outside our database. We use it only to stop someone we have banned from joining again with a new account. It is deleted with your account. If we ban you, a copy with no link to you is kept for 3 years, and a new sign-up with the same code is held for a person to review.
- **Board views**: one record per person, city and day you opened that city's board. Used, in totals, to understand whether Galley is useful. Kept 18 months.
- **Automatic account decisions**: an account reported as "not crew" is asked to verify again; an account with two open reports is suspended until reviewed; invites and message requests from an account pause if 3 or more different people block or report it within 30 days, or if fewer than 10% of at least 12 of its invites across 3 or more cities are accepted ("not now" on its own never pauses anyone), until a person reviews it; if someone you invited is rejected, your invite link is switched off, and if three are, you are asked to verify again. A person reviews every report, and you can appeal any decision (Terms, section 8).
- **Meetup records**: who joined, who said "still in", and whether two participants confirmed it happened. Used for go/no-go, reminders and to measure whether meetups happen.
- **Product analytics**: events such as "signed up", "set Up for" or "joined a meetup", linked to a random account ID, with counts and chip names. No name, email, airline, message text, city or meetup audience is sent with them, and PostHog's location lookup from your IP address is switched off. Some events carry the ID of the meetup or tip concerned. Processed by PostHog in the EU.
- **Technical data**: IP address, device type, operating system and app version, which our hosting and update providers log to run and secure the service.

### 3.3 Data we do not collect

Where you stay (hotel names or addresses), room numbers, flight numbers, pairings, duty times, staff or crew ID numbers (only the one-way identity mark above), passport or licence numbers, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, health information, political views, contacts from your phone, and your location in the background. A plan at your base can name its venue, including a hotel's courts, gym or restaurant, with that venue's address and pin; that is the place you meet, shown only to people who join. Searching for a venue never uses your phone's location. Location is used only while the app is open, in two cases: if you choose to pin a tip to where you are standing (rounded to about 100 metres), and when you send an invite to someone who takes invites only from crew who've landed. In that case your phone checks, using approximate location, that it is within about 50 km of the city, and tells us only the city.

We never run face recognition or any face-matching software. A selfie sent with a crew ID is compared by eye by a person on our team, then deleted.

## 4. Meetup photos

Only someone who was in a meetup can add a photo, and only after it starts. The photo is private to the people who were in that meetup when it was added. Each of them has a switch, "Show on the city page", and the photo appears on the city page only when every one of them has switched it on. Any of them can switch it off at any time, which removes it from the city page immediately, and any of them can delete the photo for everyone. If someone in the photo was not a member of the meetup, or you want a photo of you removed after the meetup has left the app (7 days after it starts), write to [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app) and we remove it within 72 hours. If anyone who was in the meetup deletes their account, every photo they were on goes back to private until everyone left agrees again. Photos keep the city, day and meetup title after the meetup record itself is deleted.

## 4a. Messages are end-to-end encrypted

Chats, meetup group chats and any older message requests are encrypted on the sender's phone before they are sent, and can be opened only by the phones of the people in the conversation. Each phone creates its own private key, which stays in the phone's secure storage and is never sent to us. We, our hosting provider and anyone who gains access to our servers see only sealed ciphertext.

What we can see: that a conversation exists, between whom, when each message was sent, its approximate size, how many phones you use and when each last opened Galley, the "Up for" choice a request started from, invites (who, the plan, the day, the time of day and the answer; invites contain no text), and anything you post to a board (meetup titles, neighbourhoods, venues with their addresses and pins, and times).

What this means: a new phone cannot open messages sent before it was signed in; deleting your account erases the private key on the phone you delete from and all our copies of your public keys, so nothing left on our servers can be opened again (other phones signed in to the account keep their key until the app is removed); your data export opens the messages you sent, on your phone.

**Reports.** Because we cannot read conversations, when you report someone the app offers to attach your recent messages with them. If you agree, your phone opens those messages and sends them to us with the single-use key for each one, which lets our reviewer confirm every attached message matches what was actually sent. Only those messages are disclosed. You can always report without attaching anything.

## 5. Who can see you

- **Before verification**: nobody but our verification team.
- **After verification**: verified members see your profile only when you share a meetup, or when you are both on a layover in the same city with overlapping dates and the overlap starts within 2 days; never if either of you has blocked the other. Before that, someone with an overlapping layover sees only how many crew overlap them, by role. When the overlap ends, you disappear from their board. A conversation you both accepted stays open.
- **At your base**: you appear on no board there, and your free days are only counted (never fewer than 3, never who). Crew based in your city see activities and how many are in each club; members of a club you're in see you in its member list unless you hide yourself there. On a day you pick something you're up for, crew at your base in the group you chose (anyone, women only or men only) see you in "Up for something" and can open your profile; pick nothing and you stay out of it. Anyone in the same activity as you can start a private chat with you once you've both joined, and so can people you did an activity with while it's still in their history. Two people at their own base can't invite each other.
- **Who can contact you**: nobody can invite you unless you switch on "Open to invites" (it starts off). When it's on, by default only crew who've landed in your city can invite you; you can widen it to anyone with an overlapping stay. Without invites, people can still meet you at events. Starting a new conversation with free text is no longer possible; it begins with an invite you accept.
- **Limits that protect you**: a stay's city can't be changed after it is added, stays can start at most 60 days ahead, each account can add 8 stays and send 6 invites or requests a week (3 waiting at a time), your home base is your base city, and your base can change once a month.
- **Only at events**: in Settings you can choose "Only at events". You then appear on no city board and not in "Up for something" at your base, and only people who share a meetup with you (or have one in their history) can open your profile or invite you. You still see others and still count in the numbers.
- **City activity**: anyone verified can see how many crew are in each city. These are counts only, never names.
- **Admins** never appear on boards or in counts.

## 7. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with airlines, employers, advertisers or data brokers. We use these providers to run Galley. Each acts on our instructions under a data processing agreement:

| Provider | What it does | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Database, sign-in, file storage, scheduled jobs | European Union |
| PostHog | Product analytics | European Union |
| Resend | Sends account and verification emails | United States |
| Expo (650 Industries) | Delivers app updates and, when enabled, push notifications | United States |
| Apple and Google | App distribution and, when enabled, push notification delivery | Their own terms apply |

When data goes to a provider in the United States, the transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses in that provider's agreement. Our team accesses data from the United Arab Emirates.

If you tap a Maps link on a tip or a plan, Apple Maps or Google Maps opens with the place name and, if it has a pin, its location. Their privacy policies then apply.

When you search for a venue, the words you type and your base city's centre go from our server to Photon (photon.komoot.io), an OpenStreetMap search run by komoot in Germany. It never receives your account, your IP address or your phone's location, and we don't keep the search. Place data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

For messages we can only ever provide ciphertext we cannot open and the metadata in section 4a. We disclose data to authorities only under valid legal process, or without it only where we believe in good faith that there is a risk of death or serious injury. Our process is published at [crewgalley.app/reporting/](/reporting/).

If Galley is transferred to a company we form to operate it (we plan to do this as it grows), your data moves with the service under this policy, and we will tell you before it happens.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. You can do most of it yourself:

- **Export**: Settings → Export my data gives you your account, profile, verifications, consents, stays, the messages you sent (opened on your phone), meetups, photos, tips, blocks and reports. For a complete copy of everything we hold about you, including records such as board views and decisions about your account, write to [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app).
- **Correct**: edit your profile, stays, tips, club posts and club places at any time.
- **Delete**: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your profile, photos, stays, messages you sent, tips, comments on tips, club posts and replies, activity suggestions, consents and verification records, and removes your files from storage, usually within 24 hours. If you cannot open the app, see [crewgalley.app/delete-account/](/delete-account/).
- **Withdraw consent**: clear your gender, switch off "Show on the city page", or remove a tip pin.
- **Analytics**: switch off in Settings.

For anything else, write to [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app) from your account email. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority: in the EU, the authority where you live; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in Brazil, the ANPD. We would rather hear from you first.

## 10. Security

Messages are end-to-end encrypted (section 4a). All other data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our hosting provider. Access is restricted by database permissions and row-level rules that decide who can read each row and call each function, tested automatically on every change. Proofs sit in a private bucket only our verification team can open, and every access will be logged. Admin access is limited to two named people. No system is perfect: if a breach puts you at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

## 11. Age

Galley is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

We will post changes here with a new version date. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email at least 30 days before it takes effect, and ask you to agree again where the law requires it.
