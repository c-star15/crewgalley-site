# How joining works

## Who it's for
Pilots and cabin crew flying now, or in airline new-hire training. Any airline, anywhere. 18 and over.

Not ground staff, dispatchers or other airline staff, family or buddy-pass holders, retired crew, student pilots, aviation fans or recruiters.

## How you join
Sign up with your personal email. Add your first name, your home base and whether you're a pilot or cabin crew, and confirm you're 18 or over and that it's true. That's it.

We don't ask which airline you fly for. No work email, crew ID, roster or staff number to join.

## What that means
Galley doesn't check that every member is crew. Everyone has told us they are and agreed to the [Terms](/terms/). Use your own judgment, the way you would with anyone new.

## The work email badge (optional)

<div class="card"><strong>Work email checked</strong>In the app's Settings, add your airline email and type the 6-digit code we send. Your profile then shows "Work email checked". It shows no airline. One email from Galley lands in your work inbox; that's all your airline's mail system sees.</div>

We keep a one-way code made from the address, never the address itself. It stops one mailbox badging two accounts and keeps a banned account from coming back with it. The badge lapses after a year. Hosts can open a plan only to members with the badge.

## When something's off
Anyone can report a profile as "not crew". After one report, that person gets 72 hours to show us two small crops from their roster (their name, and one day flown in the last 2 weeks), cropped on their phone so nothing else leaves it. They stay visible while they answer. After reports from two different people, they're hidden at once until it checks out. They never learn who reported.

We answer reports within 5 business days, and within 24 hours when it's harassment or about a plan that hasn't happened yet.

You answer for who you invite: if someone who joined with your code is banned, your code stops working.

## What this is not
Nothing here checks anyone's character. Meet in public places, tell a friend where you're going, and read [tips for meeting safely](/safety/).
