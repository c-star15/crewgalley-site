# Only real crew. Checked by a person.

## Why
You land somewhere for a day or two and your crew has other plans. Someone from another airline is in town too, free and keen to do the same thing. Galley helps you find each other.

It's much easier to say yes when you know they're really crew. That's why a person checks every account before it can see anyone.

## Who can join
Pilots and cabin crew flying now, or in airline new-hire training. Any airline, anywhere.

Galley is just for crew, so it isn't open to ground staff, dispatchers or other airline staff, family or buddy-pass holders, retired crew, student pilots, aviation fans or recruiters. If you don't have a roster, you can't join.

## How to prove it
Send one of these, whichever is easiest for you.

<div class="card"><strong>1. Roster screenshot (best)</strong>Your roster app or crew portal, with your name and airline. Hide anything else. No ID number.</div>
<div class="card"><strong>2. Company email</strong>A screenshot of your work inbox showing your name and the airline email address. Checked by a person.</div>
<div class="card"><strong>3. Crew ID + selfie</strong>Your crew ID with the number covered, plus a selfie to match the face. Only if 1 and 2 don't work.</div>

We never ask for a passport, a government ID or any ID number.

## Your proof
Only our two-person team sees it. We never share it or use it for anything else, and we delete it within 24 hours of the decision. We keep only the decision: approved, rejected or "send another proof", with the date and the reason.

## Timing
An answer within 24 hours, usually faster. If it is unclear, we ask for a second proof and tell you why.

## Keeping it crew only
You verify again every 12 months. You get an invite link and you vouch for who you invite: one rejected invite turns your link off, three put you back under review. Any profile can be reported as "not crew". That account is hidden until a new proof is approved. A person reads every report.

## What verification is not
Verification shows that someone is crew. It can't tell you what they're like as a person, so meet people the way you'd meet any new colleague. Here are some [tips for meeting safely](/safety/).
