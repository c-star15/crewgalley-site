# Instagram media

Rendered posts for @crewgalley, hosted here so the scheduler (Metricool) can fetch them by URL.
Not part of the website build (`build.py` doesn't copy this folder). Sources: the design canvas
"Galley Instagram starter kit".

- `ads/2026-10-28/`: ads, activities series (7 plans other than padel: yoga, run, live music, coffee, beach, Hatta day trip, study), each as a 3:4 post (`ad-N-*-post.jpg`, Metricool drafts) and a 9:16 story (`ad-N-*-story.jpg`). Sources in `src/`: `make_ads.py` writes the canvas artboards (captions and alt text are in its table; `--posts` prints them), `render.py` renders the JPEGs.
- `week1/`: week 1 feed and stories (28 Oct to 4 Nov) after Victor's 28 Oct decisions: post 1 reworked to "Life beyond the roster." (`p1-life-beyond-the-roster.jpg`), and every app card or screen labelled "Illustration" (`p2b-brunch-illustration.jpg`, `p3c-base-illustration.jpg`, `ad-N-*-{post,story}-illustration.jpg`). Ads render with `ads/2026-10-28/src/render.py --out ../../../week1 --suffix=-illustration`; posts 1 to 3 come from the canvas artboards `v2-1-find`, `v2-4b-chat`, `v2-3c-tuesday` (static HTML, system Inter).
- `art/`: city mosaic art, Horizon and Departures (3:4 post stills, 14 s 9:16 Reels and their covers; art and wordmark only, no copy). Re-render with `art/src/` (`shoot.py` for a still, `render.py` for a Reel).
- `reels/week1/`: the seven week 1 Reels (28 Oct-3 Nov), `w1-<day>-<name>.mp4` with `-cover.jpg`: Horizon and Departures art with new music, and five rebuilt Reels (Off today?, 47 messages, Off on a Tuesday?, Roster roulette, Clubs) with the app in light mode, an Illustration label on every app mock and the end card "Life beyond the roster." / "Opening soon". Music and effects are original (numpy synthesis); all seven are music and on-screen text. Sources in `src/`: `reels.js` (scenes, `render(t)`), `render.py` (Playwright frames), `music.py` (soundtrack), `make.py` (builds all seven at -14 LUFS).
- `week1/` opener as approved for 28 Oct (Victor, 28 Oct 15:41: Base and Clubs, Dubai soft launch): post 1 `p1-life-beyond-the-roster.jpg`; post 2 is now Clubs, `p2-clubs-1-illustration.jpg` and `p2-clubs-2-illustration.jpg` (stills of the Clubs Reel at 3.8 s and 7.6 s, `reels/week1/src/render.py stills clubs`, cropped to y 190-1630); post 3 slide 3 `p3c-base-v2.jpg` has the app's current day strip (selected = dark fill only, free = soft amber fill and amber weekday, no outline or bar). The group-chat post (47 messages) leaves the opener; its Reel stays.
- `week1/p3d-any-airline.jpg` (28 Oct, open sign-up): post 3 slide 4 with "Crew only." replaced by "Any airline." (Galley no longer promises crew only; same slide otherwise, Inter Display Black matched to line 1). Made from `2026-10-27/p03d-tuesday-crew-only.jpg`, which is retired.
