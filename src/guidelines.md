# Galley Community Guidelines

*Version 2026-10-27. Takes effect when Galley launches on the App Store and Google Play.*

Galley exists so that nobody has to spend a day off or a layover alone unless they want to. It works only if everyone here is crew, everyone is decent, and nobody has to worry about where they sleep tonight. These are the house rules. Breaking them can get your content removed or your account suspended or banned (see "What happens when someone breaks them").

## Be who you say you are

- One account, yours, with your own photos and your real first name or the name crew know you by.
- Verify with your own work email. Only vouch for people you know fly for your airline now: your account answers for it. In a crew check, crop your own roster, nothing edited.
- If you stop flying for an airline, stop using Galley. Former crew are welcome back when they return to the line.

## Protect the crew hotel

The single most important rule.

- Never post or ask for hotel names, addresses, room numbers or "which floor". Meet in the lobby or at the venue. (A plan at your base can be at a hotel's courts, gym or restaurant. That's a venue, not where crew sleep.)
- Never post flight numbers, pairings, rosters, duty times or crew lists, yours or anyone else's.
- Never share where another member is staying or when they are flying, inside or outside Galley.
- Galley blocks some of this automatically. The filter is a backstop, not permission to try.

## Respect each other

- No harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking or pressure. "No", silence and a declined request are answers. Do not create new ways to contact someone who has declined or blocked you.
- Plans, not pickup lines. Invites are for doing something together: a run, dinner, a walk. "Not now" is a full answer; don't invite the same person again or look for another way to reach them.
- At your base, people are your colleagues too. You'll see who's free on a day off so you can plan together, never where they are. Don't use it to track someone's roster or when they're away from home.
- Someone showing they're up for coffee is up for a coffee plan, not a chat-up line. Start a plan they can join.
- You can message anyone in a plan with you. Keep it to the plan and getting there; if they don't answer, that's an answer. Don't join a plan just to message someone in it.
- Add the layovers you actually fly, and don't fake your location. Stays added to see who is in a city you are not going to are misuse. The app limits each account to 8 new stays and 6 invites or requests a week, and shows names only from 2 days before an overlap.
- No hate or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, airline, rank or role.
- No sexual content, nudity, or sexual messages that weren't clearly welcomed. Galley is not a dating app. If two adults hit it off, that's their business; uninvited advances are not.
- No humiliating, mocking or rating other crew, their airlines or their bodies.
- Pilots and cabin crew are equal here. Leave rank on the aircraft.

## Respect your employer, and everyone else's

- No confidential company information: internal memos, safety reports, incident details, commercial information, crew-room gossip about named people.
- No uniforms, airline logos or IDs in your photos.
- Don't speak for an airline or present a meetup as official.
- Club member lists are for finding people to play, run or study with. Join a plan or add a tip; don't use the list to chase someone who isn't interested.
- Tapped In on a "Who's in?"? Then you're in the plan: show up, or leave it in time so someone else can take the spot.
- In a club's Tips, share what you've tried yourself: places, routes, what's worth knowing.
- In clubs, share public information and your own experience. No confidential airline documents, protected selection or interview questions, proprietary training manuals, or operational instructions. What crew share in Galley is never official airline guidance.
- Follow your own company's rules on social media, rest, alcohol before duty and fitness for duty. "Bottle to throttle" is your responsibility, not the meetup host's.

## Meet well

- Turn up when you say you're in. If plans change, tap "Leave" (or "Can't make it" in the last hour) so the seat goes back. Hosts: cancel early rather than leave people waiting.
- Choose public places. On a layover, meet in the lobby, then go together. At your base, meet at the venue.
- No pressure to drink, stay out, spend, or go anywhere private.
- Respect audience settings. Women-only plans are for women, men-only plans for men, and so on.
- Share plan links with crew you know. Any verified crew member who gets the link can join that plan, so don't post links publicly. If you're joining a plan in another city, only say you're in if you'll be there.
- Ask before taking photos of people, and before posting them anywhere outside Galley.

## Keep it real and legal

- No spam, selling, promotion, recruitment, MLM, crypto pitches, surveys or fundraising.
- No fake tips or paid reviews. Tips, club places and routes are honest recommendations from crew who have been there. Keep prices and details current, or delete them when they're wrong.
- No content that is illegal where you post it or where it is seen, including content about drugs, weapons or anything else that local law prohibits. Laws differ between layover cities; when in doubt, leave it out.
- No content involving anyone under 18. Any sign of a minor in a sexual or exploitative context is reported to the authorities.
- Don't post other people's copyrighted photos or text.

## Clubs

- Any member can ask for a club at their base. A person checks every new club before it opens, and every later change to its name or line; until then only you see it, and an open club keeps its old name.
- A club is something to do together, open to all crew at that base, and not already there.
- No clubs about dating, selling or promoting anything, or work (rosters, pay, what happens inside an airline).
- No airline, hotel or people's names in a club's name or line.
- No clubs about religion, politics, health, sexuality, ethnicity or unions: the clubs you're in show on your profile. Language clubs ("Portuguese speakers") are fine.
- If a club or a change isn't approved, you get the reason in the app and by email. Change it and send it again, remove it, or appeal. A club that isn't approved is deleted 6 months after the decision.
- Organizers keep their club on topic and can hide posts that break these guidelines.

## If something goes wrong

- **Not now** turns down an invite. The sender never finds out. Right after it you can choose **Don't show me them again** (a block) or **Report**.
- **Block** anyone, any time. You disappear from each other's boards, profiles and messages, and they can't join a plan you're in.
- **Report** someone from their profile or from your chat with them. You can attach your recent messages with them; we can't read chats otherwise, because they're encrypted. Reporting also blocks. The person isn't told who reported them.
- **Report** a club post, reply, place or route from its ••• menu. A person reviews it, and three reports from different members hide it until then. Reporting content doesn't block or suspend its author.
- **Report** a city tip or a comment on it from its ••• menu, in the same way.
- For a photo or a plan, email [reporting@crewgalley.app](mailto:reporting@crewgalley.app). In-app reporting for these is coming.
- If you are in danger, call local emergency services first, then tell us.
- Not a member? Email [reporting@crewgalley.app](mailto:reporting@crewgalley.app).

## What happens when someone breaks them

- Reports of sexual, hateful or threatening content, or of someone at risk, are acted on within 24 hours. Every other report gets a decision within 72 hours.
- Depending on what happened, we may dismiss the report, warn the person, remove content, ask them to verify again, suspend the account until we've reviewed it, or ban it permanently. Serious first offences (threats, sexual harassment, sharing someone's hotel, fake proof) can lead straight to a ban. A ban follows the person, not the account: a new account with the same work email is caught for 3 years.
- Some steps are automatic. Public text matching common hotel brands or the patterns of flight numbers, room numbers or long numbers is rejected. A "not crew" report opens a crew check: the person has 72 hours to show us two roster crops or do a short video call, and stays visible meanwhile; with no answer, or no confirmation, they're hidden until a check passes. Report "not crew" only when you have a real reason. There's no limit, but we look at the reports of anyone who files a lot of them. Two open reports suspend an account until we review it. If several people block or report someone, or very few of their invites are accepted, their invites pause until we review it.
- If we act on your account or content, we tell you what we did, which rule, whether it was automatic, and how to appeal, in the app (Settings → Account notices) and by email. Reply to that email within 6 months to appeal; we answer within 7 days.

Thanks for looking after each other. That's the whole point.
