# Open this on your phone

Password reset links open inside the Galley app, so they only work on a phone where Galley is installed.

1. Open the email on that phone.
2. Tap the link. It opens Galley on the new-password screen.

If it still opens a browser, request a new link from the app's sign-in screen; links expire after an hour.
