# Your messages are yours

Chats on Galley are end-to-end encrypted. Your phone locks each message before it's sent, and only the phones in the conversation can open it. We can't read your chats.

We do see what it takes to run the app: who you talk to and when, and anything you post in a plan or on your profile.

If you report someone, you can choose to attach messages from that chat. Only the messages you attach are shared with us.

Full details are in section 4a of our [privacy policy](/privacy/). Questions: [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app).
