# Support

Email [support@crewgalley.app](mailto:support@crewgalley.app). We answer within two working days; verification and safety reports faster.

## Common questions

**My verification is pending.** Decisions come within 24 hours. If it has been longer, email us with the address you signed up with.

**I was rejected and I am crew.** Reply to the rejection reason by email with a different proof (company email code, roster screenshot, or crew ID with the number covered plus a selfie). The admin team can reopen a review.

**Password reset.** Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen and open the link from the email on the phone that has Galley installed. If it opened in a browser instead, see [/reset/](/reset/).

**Delete my account.** In the app: You → Settings → Delete my account. It removes your profile, photos, stays, messages and verification history. If you cannot sign in, email us from the account's address.

**My data.** You can request a copy of everything the app holds about you by email; we send it within 30 days. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

**Legal and takedown requests.** [legal@crewgalley.app](mailto:legal@crewgalley.app). Requests are honoured on valid legal process only and are logged.
