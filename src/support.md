# Support

Stuck, or something not working? Email [support@crewgalley.app](mailto:support@crewgalley.app) and a person will reply within two working days. Safety reports: [reporting@crewgalley.app](mailto:reporting@crewgalley.app).

## Common questions

**My verification is pending.** Decisions come within 24 hours. If it has been longer, email us with the address you signed up with.

**I was rejected and I am crew.** Email us from your account's address with a different proof (roster screenshot, company mailbox screenshot, or crew ID with the number covered plus a selfie). A person will look again within 7 days.

**Password reset.** Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen and open the link from the email on the phone that has Galley installed. If it opened in a browser instead, see [/reset/](/reset/).

**Delete my account.** In the app: your photo at the top right → Settings → Delete my account. It removes your profile, photos, stays, messages and verification history. If you cannot sign in, email [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app) from the account's address. See [/delete-account/](/delete-account/).

**My data.** In the app: Settings → Export my data. For anything more, email [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app); we reply within 30 days. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

**Legal and takedown requests.** [legal@crewgalley.app](mailto:legal@crewgalley.app). Requests are honoured on valid legal process only and are logged.
