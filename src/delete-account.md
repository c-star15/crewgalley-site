# Delete your Galley account

*Takes effect when Galley launches on the App Store and Google Play.*

## In the app (instant)

1. Open Galley, tap your photo at the top right, then **Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and your files are removed from storage, usually within 24 hours. You can download a copy of your data first from **Settings → Export my data**.

## Without the app

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, email **[privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app)** from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm the request with you and complete it within 30 days, usually within a few days. If you've lost access to that email address, tell us and we'll find another way to confirm it's you.

## What is deleted

- your account, email address and password
- your profile, photos, bio, prompts, interests and gender if you set it
- your verification records, your identity mark, your vouch requests and the vouches you gave, and any crew-check crops that haven't been purged yet
- your stays, home base and Up for choices
- your conversations (for both people) and the messages you sent in meetup chats
- the meetups you hosted, your tips and votes, the meetup photos you uploaded, and your answers on other people's photos
- your consents and city requests
- your product analytics events
- your random account ID in our records of whether meetups happened, and your board views
- reports and blocks you made, and reports and blocks about you

## What remains, and for how long

- **Backups** are overwritten within 7 days.
- **Service logs** (IP addresses and request times) are kept by our hosting provider for no more than 90 days.
- **Copies other people already have**, such as a message you sent that they screenshotted, are outside our control.
- **Meetup photos someone else uploaded** that show you stay with the people who were there, but every one of them goes back to private until the remaining people agree again. You can ask us to remove a photo of you at [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app).
- **If your account was banned**, a one-way code made from your work email (for accounts verified before 26 October 2026, from your airline, staff number and surname), with no link to you, is kept for 3 years so the ban still applies to a new account.
- **Records we must keep by law**, such as a response to a legal request, are kept only as long as the law requires.

## Deleting some data, not everything

You can delete individual photos, tips, stays, meetups you host and conversations (for yourself) in the app at any time, without deleting your account. For anything else, write to [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app).
