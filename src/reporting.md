# Reporting content and legal requests

*Takes effect when Galley launches on the App Store and Google Play.*

Galley is operated by Galley's founder, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. This page is for members, non-members, rights holders and authorities.

## Report something on Galley

**Members:** use **Report** on someone's profile or in your chat with them; you can attach your recent messages. Reporting also blocks the person.

**Anyone else:** email **[reporting@crewgalley.app](mailto:reporting@crewgalley.app)** with:

1. what the content or account is (a screenshot and the name shown in the app help us find it);
2. why you think it is illegal or breaks our Community Guidelines;
3. your name and email address. You may leave these out when reporting child sexual abuse material;
4. a statement that you believe the report is accurate and complete.

We confirm receipt, act within 24 hours on sexual, hateful or threatening content or someone at risk, decide everything else within 72 hours, and tell you what we decided. If we remove content or restrict an account, the person affected is told why and how to appeal. We do not tell them who reported them unless the law requires it.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

## Copyright

If you believe content on Galley infringes your copyright, email **[legal@crewgalley.app](mailto:legal@crewgalley.app)** with: the work you own, where the infringing content is on Galley, your contact details, a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised, a statement that the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for them, and your physical or electronic signature. We remove infringing content promptly and close repeat infringers' accounts. A member whose content is removed can send a counter-notice to the same address.

## Requests from law enforcement and authorities

Galley holds little: no hotels, flights or precise locations; messages are end-to-end encrypted, so we hold only ciphertext we cannot open and the metadata in Privacy Policy section 4a; work email addresses are not kept, and crew-check crops are deleted soon after a decision; stays, Up for and meetups expire within days. Some records last longer (accepted conversations' metadata, 18 months of board views and meetup records). Section 8 of the Privacy Policy lists every period. Please check whether what you need could exist before you ask.

- Send requests to **[legal@crewgalley.app](mailto:legal@crewgalley.app)** from an official address, identifying the authority, the legal basis, the account concerned (email or display name and airline), and exactly what is requested.
- We disclose data only under valid legal process that binds us. We may ask for the request to come through an international channel (for example a mutual legal assistance request) where the law requires.
- We tell the member about the request before disclosing, unless the law prohibits it or doing so would put someone at risk.
- We push back on requests that are overbroad, unclear or not legally valid.
- **Emergencies:** if there is a risk of death or serious physical injury, mark the email "EMERGENCY" and explain the risk. We may disclose what is necessary without legal process where we believe in good faith that it will prevent that harm.
- On a valid preservation request we copy the specific data that still exists at that moment and hold it for 90 days, renewable once, while you obtain legal process. Data already deleted under our retention periods cannot be recovered.

Every request and our response are logged. We plan to publish a yearly count of requests received and answered.
