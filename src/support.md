# Support

Stuck, or something not working? Email [support@crewgalley.app](mailto:support@crewgalley.app) and a person will reply within two working days. Safety reports: [reporting@crewgalley.app](mailto:reporting@crewgalley.app).

## Common questions

**How do I join?** Sign up with your personal email, then add your name, base and whether you're a pilot or cabin crew. No airline, no work email, no ID. See [how joining works](/verification/).

**The badge code didn't arrive.** The work email badge is optional. Check spam; codes work for 15 minutes and you can ask for 3 a day. If your airline's email isn't one we know yet, email us and we'll add it.

**I was hidden after a "not crew" report.** Open the app and do the crew check: two small crops from your roster, cut on your phone. A person looks within a day. Still stuck? Email us from your account's address. We never ask for ID.

**Password reset.** Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen and open the link from the email on the phone that has Galley installed. If it opened in a browser instead, see [/reset/](/reset/).

**Delete my account.** In the app: your photo at the top right → Settings → Delete my account. It removes your profile, photos, stays, messages and account history. If you cannot sign in, email [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app) from the account's address. See [/delete-account/](/delete-account/).

**My data.** In the app: Settings → Export my data. For anything more, email [privacy@crewgalley.app](mailto:privacy@crewgalley.app); we reply within 30 days. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

**Legal and takedown requests.** [legal@crewgalley.app](mailto:legal@crewgalley.app). Requests are honoured on valid legal process only and are logged.
